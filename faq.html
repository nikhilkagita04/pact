Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Pact

General Questions

When will Pact be available?

Pact is launching in Q4 2025 on both iOS and Android. The first 100 users who join our waitlist will receive lifetime premium access for free!

Is Pact free to use?

Yes! Core features are completely free. Premium features include AI-powered suggestions, advanced reminder settings, and detailed analytics. The first 100 users get lifetime premium access at no cost.

What devices will Pact work on?

Pact will be available on iOS and Android mobile devices. We're focusing on mobile-first design to make it easy to capture commitments in the moment.

Is this only for couples?

Not at all! Pact works for any relationship where commitments matter—roommates, family members, friends, or partners. It's designed to strengthen any connection through kept promises.

Features & Functionality

How is Pact different from regular to-do apps?

Pact is designed specifically for shared commitments, not personal tasks. It focuses on the relationship aspect—turning casual promises into clear, shared commitments with gentle reminders and celebration when completed.

Can I use Pact with multiple people?

Yes! You can create different "pacts" with different people or groups. Whether it's your roommate, family, or close friends, each relationship gets its own space.

What if someone forgets to complete their commitment?

Pact sends gentle, friendly reminders that feel less like nagging and more like helpful nudges. The goal is to support follow-through, not create pressure.

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. Your personal commitments are private and encrypted. We never share your data with third parties, and you can delete your account and data at any time.

Technical Questions

Do I need an internet connection to use Pact?

Pact requires an internet connection to sync commitments between users. However, you can view your commitments offline once they're downloaded to your device.

What if I want to stop using Pact?

You can delete your account and all your data at any time. We make it easy to leave if Pact isn't right for you, though we hope you'll love it!

Will Pact work internationally?

Yes! Pact will be available worldwide, though initially in English. We're planning to add more languages based on user demand.
