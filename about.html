About Pact

Building trust through kept commitments

Our Mission

At Pact, we believe that trust is the foundation of every great relationship, and trust is built on kept promises. In our busy lives, small commitments—the threads that weave our relationships together—get lost in texts and mental to-do lists, creating quiet friction.

We're building a more intentional way to handle these commitments, something that feels less like a task manager and more like a digital handshake.

The Problem We're Solving

Every day, we make small promises to the people we care about:

  • "I'll pick up groceries on my way home"
  • "I'll call the plumber about that leak"
  • "I'll take care of the dishes tonight"
  • "I'll help you move this weekend"

These aren't just tasks—they're commitments that strengthen our connections. But when they get forgotten or lost in text threads, they create quiet disappointment and missed opportunities for connection.

Our Solution

Pact is designed to feel less like a to-do list and more like a digital handshake. It's a quiet, shared space to turn good intentions into reliable actions that strengthen your connections.

We're creating a relationship app designed to replace the anxiety of forgetting with the quiet confidence of following through, helping you spend less energy managing commitments and more energy strengthening the connections that matter most.
